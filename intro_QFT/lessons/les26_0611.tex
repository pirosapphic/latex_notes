\subsection{Counting divergences}
Let $d$ be the number of spacetime dimensions: a \textit{superficial degree of
divergence} is
$$D = dL-2I$$
where $L$ is the number of loops and $I$ is the number of internal lines.\\
If $D>0$, the integral \textit{diverges} as a $D$-power.\\
If $D = 0$, the integral \textit{diverges} logarithmically.\\
If $D<0$ the integral \textit{converges}.\\
After some calculations, for a $\phi^N$ theory
$$D = d-\frac{d-2}{2}E +V\left(\frac{N-2}{2}d-N\right)$$
where $E$ is the number of external lines and $V$ that of vertices.
